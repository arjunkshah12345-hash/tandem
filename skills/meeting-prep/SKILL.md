# Meeting prep

Produce a single markdown artifact titled `Prep — {meeting} ({date})`. Aim for something readable in two minutes.

## Research
1. **Attendees** — for each external person: role, company, how the user knows them (search email history and memory first, then the web for public professional info only). Never look up personal details like home address or family.
2. **History** — last 3–5 email threads with these people or their domain; previous meetings on the calendar; any notes in memory.
3. **Open items** — unanswered questions in either direction, promises made ("I'll send…"), invoices or contracts in flight.
4. **Company context** (first meetings) — what they do, size, recent news from the last 90 days. Cite links.

## Brief structure
```
# Prep — Intro call with Brightline (Thu 13:00)
**Goal (suggested):** decide whether to run a paid pilot.
**Who:** Dana Kim — Head of Ops at Brightline (40 people, logistics SaaS). Introduced by Sam on Oct 3.
**History:** One email exchange; Dana asked about SOC 2 and pricing for 10 seats.
**Open items:** You promised a security overview — not sent yet.
**Talking points:** 1) … 2) … 3) …
**Watch out for:** budget timing (their FY starts Jan).
**Sources:** [links]
```
Keep facts and guesses separate: mark anything inferred with "(likely)". After creating it, post one line in the thread with a link to the artifact.
